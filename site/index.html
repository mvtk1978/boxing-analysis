Chin Up — Head Exposed 100.0%

Confirmed across all three analysis runs. Jose's chin is elevated for the entire fight — chin_elevation metric exceeds the critical threshold (>0.20 normalized body height) in every clean frame. He never tucks his chin, leaving the jaw as a permanent target for hooks and uppercuts.

How Otar exploits this: The exposed chin is THE money shot opportunity. Left hook to the chin behind the jab. Uppercuts through the guard land clean every time. Time overhand rights when he's stationary on the line.
Lead Hand Too Low 84.4%

Lead hand drops below the guard threshold in 84% of clean frames. Direct path to his face for the right hand, no parry ability. Combined with the chin-up issue, this is a guaranteed entry for power shots up the middle.

How Otar exploits this: Straight right and overhand right have a clean lane. Jab to draw the lead hand down further, then come over the top. No check hook defense when the hand drops.
Stance Too Narrow 80.0%

Feet too close together in 80% of clean frames. Narrow base = no balance under pressure, weak power transfer, can't absorb body shots. (Note: v2 briefly read this as 2% because the contaminated Valdez segment had different stance characteristics that washed out the real signal.)

How Otar exploits this: Aggressive pressure and combination punching. Narrow base means any significant shot rocks him off balance. Body shots fold him — this is exactly how Conceição finished him in R7. Push him to the ropes; he can't escape from there.
Rear Hand Too Low 77.8%

Rear hand also drops 78% of the time. Combined with the lead hand low (84%), Jose has BOTH hands low for most of the fight — meaning the entire guard collapses. The chin tuck does nothing without hand cover, and there's no defense above the chest line.

How Otar exploits this: When both hands are low, EVERY punch type is open. Lead hook to the temple, straight right to the chin, uppercut up the middle. Volume punching will overwhelm — he has no way to cover up effectively.
Lead Elbow Flared 68.9%

Lead elbow wings out in 69% of frames, leaving his left side body completely exposed and reducing guard structure during and after his own punches.

How Otar exploits this: Counter with the right hand over his flared lead elbow. When his elbow lifts, step right and throw the straight down the pipe. Right hooks to the body under the elbow create body damage, and he won't be able to defend the rib/liver line.
Rear Elbow Flared 57.8%

Rear elbow drifts away from his body in 58% of frames, opening a corridor to his liver and ribs on the right side. The elbow angle exceeds 110 degrees frequently.

How Otar exploits this: Left hooks to the liver land through this gap consistently. This is exactly how Conceição finished him — relentless body work through the flared rear elbow opening, ending in the round 7 body shot KO. Feint high, dig low.
