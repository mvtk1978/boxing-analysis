Jose Guardado — Fault Detection v4 — GUARDADO ONLY

10,475 frames from the actual Conceição vs Guardado bout (1:38 bell to 8:37 end). Guardado-specific filter: Conceição (green trunks) and referee (blue shirt) excluded. 7,350 confirmed Guardado poses analyzed. Each flaw has its own embedded reel with 4 best examples in slow motion.

Lead Hand Too Low 100.0%

Guardado's #1 vulnerability. His lead hand is below the guard threshold in EVERY clean frame of the fight. Direct path to his face for the right hand, no parry ability whatsoever. The lead hook to his chin and the straight right both have completely uncontested lanes.

How Otar exploits this: Jab to measure, then come over the top with the straight right or overhand right. He has zero check-hook defense. The longer the fight goes, the more this opens up — he doesn't recover his guard between exchanges.
Lead Elbow Flared 88.9%

Lead elbow flared in 89% of frames — nearly always. His left side body is wide open: ribs, liver area on his lead side, and the corridor for the right hand to come over.

How Otar exploits this: Right hooks to the body under the lead elbow create the body damage that breaks him in later rounds. Straight right over the top of the flared elbow is a free shot. When he tries to throw his jab, his elbow flares even higher — that's the moment to step right and counter.
Rear Hand Too Low 86.7%

Rear hand drops in 87% of frames. Combined with the 100% lead-hand-low, Jose's ENTIRE GUARD is down most of the fight. He's basically fighting with his hands at his hips. There's no protection above the chest line and no parry capability for either side.

How Otar exploits this: Volume punching to the head. Lead hook → cross → uppercut combinations. He has no way to cover up effectively because both hands need to travel huge distances back to a guard position. Combination volume is the path to TKO.
Rear Elbow Flared 62.2%

Rear elbow flared in 62% of frames. Opens the liver / right-ribs corridor — precisely where Conceição's body shot landed for the round 7 KO.

How Otar exploits this: Left hook to the liver. This is the kill shot that Conceição used to finish him. Feint high to draw his eyes, then dig the left hook through the rear-elbow gap. Short hooks to the body in clinches and on the inside also land.
Stance Too Narrow 48.9%

Narrow stance in roughly half of clean frames. Less consistent than the guard issues, but creates moments of weak balance — especially when he's pressed or pivoting.

How Otar exploits this: When his feet come together, push hard with combinations — he can't brace. Pressure to the ropes amplifies this; with a narrow base he can't pivot away cleanly. Body shots in those moments fold him.
Chin Up — Head Exposed 44.4%

Chin up in 44% of clean frames. Much lower than earlier readings (v1-v3 had this at 64-100%) — that data was largely Conceição, who keeps his chin high more often. Guardado actually does some chin tucking, but combined with no hand-guard, the moments he doesn't tuck are still very exploitable.

How Otar exploits this: Time the moments his chin floats up — usually when he's resetting or after he throws. Uppercut up the middle through his weak guard, or hook to the chin off the jab. Less critical than the hands/elbow issues but still a clean opportunity.
Trunk Leaning Backward 4.4%

Pulls back to evade in 4% of frames. Minor. When it happens, his power is gone and he's vulnerable to step-in counters.

How Otar exploits this: When he leans back, step in with the jab to close the gap, then unload — he can't generate power to counter from that position.

Fight Recap: Conceicao vs Guardado

April 13, 2024 | American Bank Center, Corpus Christi, TX | TKO Round 7

What Conceicao Did Right

  • Established the jab early and used it constantly to control distance
  • Attacked the body relentlessly through Guardado's flared elbows
  • Maintained aggressive pressure, never let Guardado set his feet
  • Landed hard left hands in succession, targeting the exposed chin
  • Built up body damage systematically until the R7 knockout shot

What Guardado Did Wrong

  • Head glued to center line - zero lateral movement or slipping
  • Chin up the entire fight - absorbed clean shots he should have avoided
  • No offensive pressure - fight described as "a sparring session"
  • Left eye swelling but made no defensive adjustments
  • Narrow stance left him unable to absorb body shots properly
  • Never established counters to disrupt opponent's rhythm

Also Lost to Victor Morales Jr (Dec 2024)

  • Lost by retirement in round 7 - same pattern of accumulating damage
  • Morales (19-0-1, orthodox, 5'7") pressured him identically to Conceicao
  • Shows Guardado has NOT fixed these technical flaws between fights
  • Vulnerable fighters who can pressure and work the body beat him consistently
